OUR STORY

Two years.

Some memories are loud. Some are tiny. Some stay in your heart without needing a reason.

This diary is for all of them.

CHAPTER 01

The Beginning

30 September 2024

Monday · 12:00 PM · Lodhi Garden

where our story quietly began

30 September 2024.

We didn't know that this ordinary day would eventually become one of the most important dates in our story.

You wanted to hug me. I said no.

And then you left with that little disappointed face before going to the airport.

But somehow, that goodbye wasn't really a goodbye.

We kept talking for hours, even into the night.

And somewhere between those conversations, jokes and random little interactions, something started becoming ours.

Maybe we didn't know it then... but this was the beginning.

🎀

CHAPTER 02

The Second Date

14 September 2025

Sunday · 3:00 PM · Green Park Metro Station

another day, another memory

I was busy with my planshow.

I was already tired from everything, and your teasing finally made me come to meet you.

I was exhausted... but you were there.

And because you were there, I knew you'd take care of me.

I finally met you at Green Park Metro Station. I was nervous, but somehow, you made me comfortable.

In the metro, I punched you and people around us started looking at us.

From Green Park, we went towards CP.

Before we even reached there, you had already taken my hand into yours.

We went to a café, but I didn't really like the food.

So I simply said, "Let's leave."

You picked up my bag, booked an auto, and we went together.

Somewhere along the way, you started teasing me again.

SAME DAY · LATER

Just Us.

♡ ✦ ♡

We reached the room and you showed me the video of your Ganesh Chaturthi performance.

We watched it together. I told you to stop using your phone.

And then there were hugs, kisses, conversations, teasing and our little fights.

The kind of moments that don't look extraordinary from outside, but somehow mean everything to us.

Later, you dropped me downstairs and arranged an auto for me.

We kept talking on the phone even after I reached home.

And yes... you even showed me your new shirt.

☕

CHAPTER 03

One More Day

15 September 2025

Monday · His Room

one more day before goodbye

The next day, I went to your room again.

We met on the way while you were getting some things.

☕ Maggi. Chai. Us. ♡

We made Maggi together. We made chai. We watched videos. We talked.

You told me about how you got your job.

You became emotional. You cried. And I hugged you.

It was one of those moments where words weren't really necessary.

Nothing extraordinary.

And yet, somehow, it felt like everything.

A LITTLE FIGHT

We didn't always get it right.

There was a misunderstanding.

I felt like you didn't love me enough and I said, "Aap pyaar nahi karte."

I cried. You became emotional too.

And eventually, we hugged and tried to understand each other again.

Because that's also us.

We fight. We feel. We talk. We make up.

And somewhere in between all of it, we still choose to stay close.

✈
♡

THE GOODBYE

And then, it was time to leave.

15 September 2025

Delhi → Pune

After everything, I finally had to go home.

You had to go to the airport.

Pune was waiting.

Your face looked like you didn't really want to leave either.

And I cried.

Because missing someone feels completely different when you have just spent precious time with them.

I missed you before you were even far away.
