THE BEGINNING

30 September 2024

Monday · 12 PM · Lodhi Garden

• • • • •

The day we didn't know was going to matter this much.

It started as an unexpected meeting at Lodhi Garden. He wanted to hug me. I refused.

He left a little disappointed and went towards the airport. But the story didn't end there.

We kept talking that night. And somehow, without forcing anything, the conversations became easier, funnier and warmer.

We developed this invisible but jolly little bond — the kind that quietly grows before you even realise how important it has become.

THE DAY WE LIVED

14 September 2025

Green Park Metro · CP · Room

GREEN PARK CP ROOM

No photograph. Just a day full of tiny moments.

Planshow had kept me busy and tired, and I was already running late. But his teasing somehow convinced me to meet him.

We met at Green Park Metro. I was nervous, but he made me comfortable almost instantly.

In the metro I punched him, and of course people looked.

Before reaching CP, he held my hand. Then came food I didn't particularly like, my classic "let's leave" moment, his carrying my bag, an auto ride together, teasing, shyness, and all those little things that don't look important from outside... but somehow become everything inside a memory.

04

THE LAST EVENING

15 September 2025

Room · Chai · Before Goodbye

✦ ✧ ✦

We knew the evening was ending. We just didn't want it to.

This time we went directly to the room. No outside plans. Just time together.

We made chai while time kept moving faster than we wanted it to.

I was fixing my hair in the bathroom, and he was watching from outside. I don't know why... but I really liked that little moment.

He was going to leave for the airport. I was going home. So we simply stayed together for as long as we could.
