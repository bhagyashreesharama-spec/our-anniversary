The Second Date

I was at L'Delton Hall because I had received an invitation, and because of that, I got late.

Then I finally met you at Green Park Metro Station.

I was nervous, but somehow you made me feel comfortable.

In the metro, I punched you and people around us started looking at us.

From Green Park, we went towards CP.

Before we even reached there, you had already taken my hand into yours.

CP & THE CAFÉ

We went to a café and talked, but I didn't really like the food.

So I simply said, “Let's leave.”

You picked up my bag, booked an auto, and we left together.

THE AUTO RIDE

You kept teasing me.

Somewhere between all the teasing, you kissed my cheek and I got shy.

LATER THAT NIGHT

When we reached the room, you showed me the video of your Ganesh Chaturthi performance.

We watched it together.

Then I told you to stop the phone and just be with me.

We hugged. We kissed. We talked. We laughed.

There were also our little fights and endless teasing.

Eventually, you walked me downstairs, arranged an auto for me, and I went home.

The next day, I was going to your room again.

We met on the way while you were getting some things.

Maggi. Chai. Us.

We made Maggi together. We made chai. We watched videos. We talked.

Nothing extraordinary.

And yet, somehow, it felt like everything.

ONE CONVERSATION

You told me about how you got your job.

While telling me everything, you became emotional.

Sometimes words aren't enough, so I just stayed close.

LATER

I felt like you didn't love me the way I wanted to be loved.

We fought. I cried. You became emotional too.

But somehow, even in the middle of the fight, we found our way back to each other's arms.

THAT NIGHT

We had Maggi.

We cuddled.

I fell asleep.

When I woke up, you made chai for me.

More talking. More hugs. More little moments.
