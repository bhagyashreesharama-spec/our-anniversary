A story we never planned

Before it became “us”

There are some days that look completely ordinary when they begin, and only later do you realize that they quietly changed everything.

For us, that day was 30 September 2024.

There was no plan to meet someone special. There was no expectation of a date. There wasn't even a thought that the person standing beside us would still matter two years later.

And yet… here we are.

Chapter One

30 September 2024

Monday · Lodhi Garden → Connaught Place

Before noon

A completely different reason for going out

I had an online digital marketing class that day, and I thought I would take it while sitting in Lodhi Garden.

At that time, my Smart Value / network marketing team had already ended. The people I knew had left, and suddenly I found myself thinking about meeting new people, making friends and building a new team.

I was bored that day, so I decided to go outside. And for the first time, I went to Lodhi Garden alone.

Around 12 PM · Lodhi Garden

The same step, the same moment

Around 12 PM, both of us arrived at almost the same time, at the same step.

You looked at me and simply said:

“Hi, I’m Kunal, I like your style.”

I was wearing a shirt-and-trouser type outfit, shoes and a bag. You were wearing a T-shirt and trousers.

I remember thinking that you might be a college student living somewhere in Delhi.

And apparently, you thought I looked like a working girl.

We talked for a little while.

Then I started walking away.

You were alone, and when it looked like I was ignoring you, your face changed a little.

I looked at the situation differently. We were both alone, and I thought, why not make him a friend?

So I went back with you.

Later · Lodhi Garden

Too many similarities to ignore

We started talking properly, and very quickly discovered that we had many similar hobbies, interests and ways of thinking.

It felt like there were more similarities than there should have been between two people who had literally just met.

It felt like more than coincidence.

But at that moment, I didn't think too much about it.

We kept walking and roaming around different places. Time passed without us noticing.

I even missed my class.

Lodhi Garden

The duck that became a “duct”

At one point, I took you towards the part of Lodhi Garden where there were ducks and water.

My throat wasn't good that day, so when I said “duck”, you heard something completely different.

Somehow, neither of us found exactly the ducks, river or waterfall we were expecting.

Our first misunderstanding… and it wasn't even a serious one. 😂

Around 2 PM · Leaving Lodhi Garden

“Let's go to CP.”

While sitting and talking, you told me that you had been to CP before and liked the mutton there.

I had never been to CP.

So you suggested going there.

You asked if I had to go home. I told you I still had time.

We were free from Lodhi Road around 2 PM. We started walking while eating kulfi and talking.

Then you booked an auto.

Of course, you were still a complete stranger to me. I felt a little uncomfortable about getting into an auto with someone I had met only that day.

But after spending those two hours together, I thought it would be okay.

The first little journey after we met.

And then, completely suddenly, you put your hand on my shoulder.

I got a little scared. I was comfortable around male friends, but you were new.

“Arey aisa waisa nahi hoon yrr, darro mat.”

And somehow, that was enough to make me stay.

Later · Connaught Place

Bad rajma, good company

We reached CP and went to eat rajma.

The rajma was honestly bad.

Neither of us liked it, but neither of us really said it to the other.

Eventually, we gave up and threw it away.

Then a very overweight uncle came by, and I casually commented:

“Kya figure hai.”

You laughed.

Somehow, even silly little moments like that became part of the day.

CP streets

The hand you didn't let go of

We crossed the road together.

You wanted to hold my hand, but instead of simply saying it, you found an excuse.

I gave you my hand.

And you just kept holding it.

Your hand felt warm.

I even told you that.

I didn't know then that you had already been taking little chances and flirting through the day.

My innocence apparently made your work easier. 🙃

Later · Café, Connaught Place

The café I don't remember the name of

We eventually went to a café.

In my head, this still wasn't a date. It was just an unexpected day out with someone I had met that afternoon.

I had never even had a date with Tarun, so the word “date” wasn't exactly something I was thinking about.

You later told me that, actually, this was a date.

Somewhere in CP, on a day that wasn't supposed to become a memory like this.

There was a girl sitting behind you who kept looking at you.

I wasn't jealous then.

But now?

“Mere maal ko dekha kaise bc.” 😂

You wanted to dance with me. I told you to dance with the girl instead.

You looked back, and maybe she finally stopped looking.

Later that afternoon

Things I didn't notice that day

I didn't really look at you carefully that day.

I didn't notice your dimple properly. I didn't notice your cute smile the way I would later.

But somehow, your voice and the conversations stayed in my mind.

We kept wandering around, looking at little things like keychains and just talking.

Later · Before the airport

A goodbye that wasn't supposed to matter so much

Then you got a call. You had to reach the airport.

I asked you to drop me at the bus stand.

You wanted to hug me.

I didn't let you.

Your rider arrived, and you left.

You looked a little upset.

After reaching home, I messaged you:

“mai ghar pohoch gyi aap kaha tak pohoche.”

And then we talked on Instagram almost the entire night.

I genuinely enjoyed talking to you.

I still didn't know that, according to you, I had already been on a date.

“

“aap phle aise insan ho jiske muh se jaida acha algta hai mera naam bhagya.”

And somehow, a name became part of the story too.

30.09.2024

One ordinary day. One unexpected meeting. One story beginning quietly.

Time passed.

And somehow, we kept choosing each other.

One year later, another September came.

Chapter Two

14 September 2025

Sunday · Delhi

Around 4 AM

“mai agya”

“After Ganesh Chaturthi and a little bit of natak… you finally landed in Delhi. ❤️”.

You reached around 4 AM.

Around 8 AM, I saw your WhatsApp:

“mai agya”

I was at Delton Hall that morning. “You were supposed to come in the morning… but you came late for the 11 AM shift, so I had to stay till 2 PM. 😭” .

I had expected to be in the 9 AM shift and be free around noon. The plan was simple: I would call you to Jor Bagh Metro and we would go together.

But that plan didn't happen.

Morning

You came to the metro anyway

You called and asked:

“Bhagya kitna time lagega?”

I told you maybe around 2 PM.

You had even gone to the metro to impress me, wearing an outfit you knew I liked.

You had properly dressed up — ho ban ke.

Some girls in the metro were staring at you.

And then you left.

Around noon

Waiting for the day to finally begin

I was still busy with the invity closing work.

I talked to the invity, sent her to the “planshow”, and slowly started becoming free.

Early afternoon

The calls that scared me.

I called you after becoming free.

You had taken a bike to the room.

The phone sound was difficult to hear while you were riding, so you didn't answer several calls.

I got scared.

Then you finally called back:

“Kya hua Bhagya itna call kiya?”

I told you I was only trying to find out where you were, but because you weren't answering, I had started worrying.

“You booked a bike, and after the rider arrived, you went to the room, checked everything, turned on the AC, and finally rested.”

I told you to eat because I was going to be late.

“

“Tumhare bina aise reh lunga par tumse mile bina kuch na khaunga na kahi ghumunga.”

And suddenly the distance between Delhi and Pune felt a little smaller.

Around 3 PM

“Aa jao na yrr…”

I was busy with the Invity closing, and finally became free around 3 PM.

On call, you started teasing me:

“Aa jao na yrr warna mai flight lekr Pune chla jaunga.”

I was tired.

But you had come from so far just to meet me, and I didn't want to hurt you by not coming.

Afternoon · Jor Bagh → Green Park

Finding each other again

I went from Jor Bagh towards Green Park Metro.

At first, I couldn't see you at the exit. I went outside.

Then you called and told me you were still inside.

So I went back in.

You had already been thinking about a hug and a kiss.

I was nervous, so I didn't really do anything.

Metro

Even the ticket had its own drama

Your ticket scanned.

Mine didn't.

You had used your phone, and apparently another ticket had to be selected or slid properly.

After a little confusion, mine finally worked too.

Even the metro wanted to make us work for it. 😭

Metro → Rajiv Chowk

A crowded metro and one protective hand

We searched for Rajiv Chowk and boarded the metro.

It was extremely crowded.

You kept me protected in the crowd, and I held your hand.

People kept looking at us.

At Rajiv Chowk, you said something that annoyed me enough for me to punch you.

People looked at us again.

Later · Connaught Place

The Marathi sentence I didn't understand

We reached CP.

While walking there, you were saying something to me in Marathi:

“Pagal ladki, jaldi chal.” “वेड्या मुली, लवकर चल!”

I didn't understand what you were saying at that moment.

You, on the other hand, seemed to be enjoying yourself while I was trying to figure out what exactly you were saying.

One person talking, the other person completely confused. 😂

CP

The art café that wasn't really the plan

You searched Google for an “art cafe”.

We went there, but it turned out to be a scam and the place wasn't good.

So much for the perfect café plan.

We still sat there for a while, holding hands.

You kept holding my hand and looking at me.

There was a couple sitting behind us being romantic.

Seeing them somehow made you even more romantic.

Later

Another food disappointment

Paneer tikka arrived.

Neither of us really liked it.

So I said we should just go to the room.

Evening

The ride back

We took an auto / cab towards the room.

You kept teasing me.

Your arm came around my waist, I held your hand, and you kissed my cheek.

I became shy.

Evening · Room

A room full of feelings

At the room, you showed me your natak video.

We lay down while watching it. I watched for a little while and then asked you to stop.

The rest of the evening became much more private and intimate.

We hugged, kissed and shared a deeply personal moment together.

Afterwards, you buttoned my shirt and fixed my hair.

“Tumhe set kar sakta hu toh baal kyu nahi.”

And somehow, that made me laugh.

Later that evening

We cuddled for a while, and I ended up resting my head in your lap.

You were quietly singing something in Marathi — a little poem that I didn't understand, but somehow enjoyed listening to.

We stayed close for a while, sharing hugs and kisses before the evening moved on.

After that, we sat together on the bed for a while.

I suddenly punched you lightly in the back just to see your reaction. And honestly, your reaction was worth watching. 😂

Later, you started telling me about your dimple. You told me how your brother used to keep asking you to smile, and would sometimes poke his finger into your dimple.

Somehow, even these little stories about you became memories I wanted to keep.

When we got up and were still talking about the shirt, I hit you playfully.

You hit me back, and we both started laughing.

The shirt you brought

The shirt you brought

We stood together, hugged and kissed again.

I asked what you were going to wear the next day.

You told me you had brought a denim-shirt-like shirt.

I asked you to try it.

You took off your black shirt and put on the other one.

I went close and fixed it for you.

You became romantic again.

I told you it looked loose and that you should open it / wear it properly.

Night

Back home

We went downstairs and you booked a cab for me.

After reaching home, I messaged:

“mai agyi ghar aap khana kha lena aur so jana.”

You slept early, around 9 PM.

I slept at home too.

14.09.2025

No photographs that day. Just memories that stayed in our heads.

Chapter Three

15 September 2025

Monday · Delhi

Morning

Another morning together

In the morning, you asked me what time I would come.

I bathed, got ready and went to Yusuf Sarai roadside to wait for you.

You called and asked where I was.

I told you I was at the same place from where I had gone home the previous day.

Later · Yusuf Sarai

“Kya lu tum btao”

You came and started buying things.

While walking through the market, you kept asking:

“kya lu tum btao”

We walked around the market together and bought things before going back to the room.

Room

“Model type”

When we entered the room, you noticed my boots.

You got happy and said I looked:

“model type.”

I didn't know a pair of boots could make you that happy.

Around 5 PM · After waking up

Black tea, sleepy eyes and three little memories

At some point, I was lying on the bed using my phone and watching reels.

You took off your T-shirt and started making tea.

Then you came and lay on my left side, teasing me romantically.

The tea was ready.

You played your natak video again and explained it to me in Hindi because the original was in Marathi.

I was honestly a little bored with the video.

While drinking tea, you also acted out parts of your natak.

You looked at me lovingly even when I was slightly bored.

All three photographs belong to this one moment — making black tea together after waking around 5 PM.

After tea

The part of you I hadn't heard before

After the tea and video, I asked you about your job and why you had never told me online why you became so emotional sometimes.

You started telling me about your journey.

While telling me everything, you cried and bowed your head.

Seeing you like that made me feel bad.

I hugged you.

For a while, everything around us became quiet.

Later

A day full of complicated feelings

We stayed close to each other for a long time.

The affection between us became more intimate, and we shared another deeply private moment together.

Afterwards, I asked you something that had been sitting somewhere inside my mind:

Did you really love me?

You said yes and asked why I was asking.

I told you that so much had happened. It had only been about a year, and somehow our relationship had already become this deep.

Later

One sentence that changed the mood

You told me that your mother might marry you to a software engineer.

And obviously…

I was not exactly happy about hearing that. 😭

Later that afternoon

When the day suddenly felt heavier

At some point, the happiness of the day became mixed with too many emotions at once.

I needed some space to get dressed and collect myself, so I asked you to go to the bathroom.

“aap bathroom.”

I didn't really explain what was happening inside my head.

I quickly got dressed and prepared to leave.

You tried to stop me.

You held me, hugged me and kept asking what had happened.

There were tears on both sides.

I left anyway.

But after some time, I came back myself.

We hugged again.

We both cried.

Sometimes love doesn't look perfect. Sometimes it looks like two people trying to understand each other through tears.

Later

Then you made Maggie

After everything became a little calmer, you made Maggie for us.

You made it in such a romantic way that I kept turning red while you were cooking.

The Maggie wasn't exactly amazing.

You said it would be better with tomato.

You pulled my cheeks and teased:

“tumhare gaal daal du kya.”

We ate from the same plate.

You lifted my face and told me you couldn't see me crying.

You called me innocent "kitni masuam si ho." "tumko koi rote huye dekhe toh ro jaye."

Even while eating, you still wanted to be close.

When I asked what was wrong, you simply said:

“kuch nhi.”
Later

The video I wasn't letting you watch

After Maggie, we lay down together and cuddled.

You showed me a video to teach me how to kiss.

There was a girl in the video.

So naturally, I covered your eyes and said:

“jab mai pas hu toh aap dusri kyu dekhoge bhala.”

We joked and talked until I eventually fell asleep.

Later

The quietest part of the day

While I was sleeping, you stayed awake beside me.

You kissed me while I slept and stayed close.

When I woke up, I had a headache.

You offered to massage my head.

I asked if you hadn't slept.

You said no.

I asked why.

And you were just looking at me.

Around 5 PM

Another cup of black tea

We got up and washed our faces.

You made black tea again.

We sat on the bed and drank it together.

After tea came another quiet, intimate moment between us.

I went to the bathroom to fix my hair.

You looked at me romantically from outside.

I liked that look.

Another hug. Another kiss. Another little moment that stayed.

Evening

The hardest goodbye

Eventually, you took me downstairs and put me in a cab / auto.

I went home.

You went from the room towards the airport.

Your face looked like you might cry.

It looked like you wanted to go back to Bhagya instead of going away.

Then you messaged me:

“Bhagya mann nhi ho rha hai mera…”

And that night, I cried too.

15.09.2025

Three photographs. One black-tea moment. A thousand feelings.

After September

Then came the distance.

Delhi became Delhi again.

Pune became Pune again.

I went back to working on my things here.

You went back to your office there.

The room, the metro rides, the bad food, the tea, the little arguments, the hugs, the airport goodbye — all of it became memories we carried separately.

But the story didn't end when the visit ended.

We just went back to loving each other from different cities.

30 September 2026

Two Years

Two years since two strangers arrived at the same step.

Two years since a simple “Hi, I’m Kunal” became the beginning of something neither of us had planned.

Since then, there have been laughs, misunderstandings, silly moments, long conversations, distance, tears, hugs, kisses, tea, bad food and countless little memories.

And somehow, through all of it, there is still this story.
