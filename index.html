OUR FIRST DATE

The unexpected meet

12:00 PM · Lodhi Garden

I still remember our first proper meeting. It was not some perfectly planned romantic beginning. It happened almost unexpectedly, and somehow that made it even more special.

You wanted to hug me that day. I refused.

You left with that disappointed little face and then went towards the airport. But somehow, that was not the end of the day.

I kept talking to you. And as if things were slowly falling into their own place, our conversations became more and more fun.

Somewhere between all those conversations, teasing and little moments, we developed this invisible but jolly bonding.

little note Maybe neither of us knew it then, but this was the beginning of us.

OUR DAY OUT

Green Park → CP → You

Green Park Metro · Connaught Place · Room

That day started with me being busy with my planshow and already feeling tired and late. I had my own plans, but your teasing somehow convinced me to come and meet you.

We met at Green Park Metro. I was nervous when I saw you, but you slowly made me comfortable.

Then came the metro ride — and yes, I punched you while we were travelling. People actually looked at us.

Before we reached CP, you held my hand. It was such a small thing, but I remember it.

We went to a café and had food, but I did not really like it. So I simply said, “Let's leave.”

You picked up my bag, booked an auto, and we sat together and left.

LATER THAT DAY

A quieter part
of the day

When we reached the room, you showed me the video of your Ganesh Chaturthi performance. We watched it together and talked about it.

At some point I told you to stop looking at your phone and actually spend time with me.

The rest of the evening became our own little world — hugs, kisses, talking, teasing, playful fights and those small moments that only make sense when it is just the two of us.

Later, you dropped me downstairs and arranged an auto for me.

Even after I reached home, we kept talking on the phone.

one tiny memory You had bought a new shirt and showed it to me. Somehow even our silly little teasing and playful hitting around the “bump” became another memory to keep.

THE NEXT DAY

No journey this time

Directly to your room · Our last evening

The next day was different. There was no long journey before meeting you. I went directly to your room.

We had our own little time together there, knowing that the evening would eventually come to an end.

It was one of those days where even ordinary things felt special because we knew our time together was running out.

OUR LAST EVENING

ONE SMALL MOMENT

The chai
before goodbye

We were making chai in the room that evening, knowing that our time together was almost over.

I was about to go home and you were about to leave for the airport.

I went into the bathroom to fix my hair, and you were outside watching me.

It was such a small moment. Nothing dramatic. But I liked it.

Dear Kunnu,
