The day we didn't expect

12:00 PM · Lodhi Garden

It was supposed to be just another day, but somehow it became the first page of our story.

We met unexpectedly at Lodhi Garden. There was something strangely familiar about being around you, even though everything was still new.

You wanted to hug me. I refused.

You left a little disappointed and went towards the airport.

But the story didn't end there.

We kept talking that night. Conversation after conversation, until an invisible but very happy bond started forming between us.

14 September 2025

The day I almost didn't come

I was busy with planshow, tired and already running late. Everything felt like a reason not to meet.

But your teasing somehow convinced me.

I met you at Green Park Metro, nervous as always. You somehow made everything feel comfortable again.

In the metro, I punched you and people looked at us. Somehow even that became one of those memories we still remember.

We went towards CP. Before we even reached, you held my hand.

The cafe wasn't really our thing, so I simply said, let's leave.

You picked up my bag, booked an auto, and sat beside me.

The teasing continued. A cheek kiss. Me getting shy. Your arm around my waist. Me telling you not to.

And then we finally reached the room.

A memory without photographs

Some moments are better kept between two people.

You showed me the video of your Ganesh Chaturthi performance.

We watched it together, talked, laughed, teased each other, and forgot about the time.

I told you to stop looking at your phone.

The rest of that evening was simply ours — hugs, affection, conversations, playful fights, and all the little things that don't need a camera.

You dropped me downstairs, arranged my auto, and even after I reached home, we continued talking on the phone.

You had bought a new shirt and showed it to me. Even the silly teasing around that little bump became a memory.

♡ No photographs here. Just a memory.

One last cup

We were making chai

We were making chai in the room that evening, knowing that our time together was almost over.

There was something so ordinary about making chai, yet that ordinary moment felt special.

I was about to go home. You were about to leave for the airport.

I don't know why, but I really liked that moment.
