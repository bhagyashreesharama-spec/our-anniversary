01

The beginning

The day we met

Date 30 September 2024
Day Monday
Time 12:00 PM
Place Lodhi Garden

I still remember that day because it was the first time two people who had only known each other through conversations finally stood in front of each other.

You wanted to hug me. I refused.

You walked away with that disappointed face, and somehow that little moment stayed in my mind.

You eventually left for the airport. But our conversations didn't end there.

We kept talking through the night, and somewhere between those random conversations, teasing and laughter, something began to feel different.

Nothing was forced. Nothing was planned. We simply kept talking.

And somehow, things started falling into place on their own.

02

One year later

14 September 2025

Date 14 September 2025
Day Sunday
Time 3:00 PM
First stop Green Park Metro Station

I was busy with my planshow. I was already tired from everything, and your teasing finally made me come to meet you.

I was exhausted, but you were there... so I knew you'd take care of me.

I reached Green Park Metro Station, and even though I was nervous, you somehow made everything feel comfortable.

We got into the metro together. At some point I punched you, and of course people around us started looking.

Before we reached CP, you held my hand.

We walked around and eventually stopped at a café. The food wasn't really what I liked, so I simply told you that we should leave.

You picked up my bag, booked an auto, and we sat together.

You kept teasing me. You kissed my cheek, and I immediately became shy.

You put your arm around my waist, and obviously I told you not to.

And then we finally reached the room.

♡

Later that evening

Just us, finally.

You showed me the video of your Ganesh Chaturthi performance. We watched it together and talked about everything and nothing.

I told you to stop using your phone, because I wanted your attention.

Then came the hugs, kisses, teasing, little fights and those private moments that belonged only to us.

You eventually came downstairs with me, arranged an auto for me, and made sure I got home safely.

Even after I reached home, we continued talking on the phone.

You had bought a new shirt and showed it to me, and naturally our usual teasing started again.

Some days don't need photographs.
The memory itself is enough.
03

The next morning

15 September 2025

Date 15 September 2025
Day Monday
Place His Room

The next day was different. There was no long journey before meeting you. I went directly to your room.

We made Maggi and chai together, watched videos, talked and simply enjoyed being around each other.

You told me about how you got your job, and somewhere in that conversation, things became emotional.

I saw you cry. I hugged you.

Later, we had our misunderstanding too. I told you that you didn't love me, because sometimes my emotions become bigger than my words.

I cried. You became emotional too. And eventually we just held each other.

There were more conversations, more hugs, more little fights, and more moments that belonged only to us.

That evening

Before our time ran out

Three photographs from our last evening together.

One tiny moment

Chai, hair, and a little more time.

We were making chai in the room that evening, knowing that our time together was almost over.

I went into the bathroom to fix my hair, and you were outside watching me.

It was such a small moment, but I liked it.

✈

And then...

Goodbye, again.

Eventually, I had to go home. You had to leave for the airport.

Neither of us really wanted that moment to arrive, but time doesn't stop just because two people wish it would.

I left with a heavy heart. You left for Pune.

And just like that, we were back to missing each other.

Until the next time.

A letter I wanted

Kunnu,

If someone asked me when our story really began, I don't think I could give them only one answer.

Maybe it started on 30 September 2024. Maybe it started during one of those endless conversations. Maybe it started somewhere between all our teasing, fights and laughter.

Whatever the exact moment was, I'm just glad it became us.

We have had beautiful days, difficult days, emotional days, and completely silly days.

But every memory has one thing in common. You were there.

And I hope when we look back at this little diary years from now, we'll laugh at how much we have changed and still recognise the two people who started it all.

Happy two years, Kunnu.

Here's to everything we've already lived, and everything still waiting for us.

— Bhagya
