30 September 2024 · Monday

The unexpected beginning

12:00 PM · Lodhi Garden

It wasn't supposed to become the beginning of our story. It was just an unexpected meeting at Lodhi Garden.

We met around noon. Everything was new, a little awkward, and a little exciting.

You wanted to hug me. I refused.

You left a little disappointed and went towards the airport.

But somehow, that wasn't the end of the day.

We kept talking afterwards. The conversations just continued and continued, almost the entire night.

Somewhere between all those conversations and random little things, an invisible but very happy bonding started growing between us.

14 September 2025

The day I almost didn't come

I was busy with planshow, already tired and getting late. Everything felt like a reason to just stay back.

But your teasing somehow convinced me to come.

We met at Green Park Metro. I was nervous, but you somehow made me comfortable very quickly.

Then came the metro ride. I punched you, and people around us started looking.

Before reaching CP, you held my hand.

We went to a café, but honestly, I didn't really like the food. So I simply said, let's leave.

You picked up my bag, booked an auto, and sat with me.

The teasing continued on the way. You kissed my cheek, I got shy, you put your arm around my waist, and I told you not to.

And then we finally reached the room.

Later that evening

Just us.

Once we reached the room, you showed me the video of your Ganesh Chaturthi performance.

We watched it together, talked, laughed, teased each other, and spent our time together.

I told you to stop looking at your phone.

Then came all those quiet moments that belong only to us — cuddling, hugs, kisses, talking, playful fights and teasing each other.

Eventually you dropped me downstairs and arranged my auto.

Even after I reached home, we continued talking on the phone.

You had bought a new shirt too, and somehow even that became another silly little memory between us.

15 September 2025

The next day

The next day was different.

There was no long journey before meeting you. I went directly to your room.

We were making chai together while the evening slowly passed.

But suddenly it became really late.

I had to leave for home, and you had to leave for the airport.

We both knew that our time together was almost over.

I was in the bathroom fixing my hair, and you were watching me from outside. For some reason, I really liked that little moment.
