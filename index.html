Chapter One

The unexpected beginning

30 September 2024 Monday · 12:00 PM Lodhi Garden

I still remember that day because I didn't know it was going to become the beginning of our story.

We met unexpectedly at Lodhi Garden. It was our first proper meeting and everything felt a little unfamiliar, a little nervous and somehow exciting at the same time.

You wanted to hug me. I refused.

You left with that disappointed face and eventually went towards the airport. But the story didn't end there.

I kept talking to you. We chatted through the night and, somewhere between those conversations, things started falling naturally into place.

We had fun talking, teasing each other and slowly developed that invisible, slightly silly and very special bonding.

“ Sometimes the most important chapters begin without any warning. ”

Chapter Two

The day you came back

14 September 2025 Green Park Metro → CP → Room

You know how sometimes you are already tired and busy with your own plans, and then one person somehow manages to make you change everything?

That was this day.

I had my planshow, I was tired and it was already getting late. But your teasing somehow convinced me to come and meet you.

We met at Green Park Metro. I was nervous again, but you somehow made me comfortable.

On the metro, I punched you and people started looking at us. Obviously, you had to tease me about it.

We went towards CP. Before we even reached there, you held my hand.

We went to a cafe, but I didn't really like the food and finally said, “Let's leave.”

You picked up my bag, booked an auto, and we sat together.

There was teasing, shyness, little moments of closeness and the kind of comfortable chaos that somehow felt completely ours.

14 September 2025

A memory that needs no photograph

When we reached the room, you showed me the video of your Ganesh Chaturthi performance.

We watched it together, talked, teased each other and I kept telling you to stop being on your phone.

The rest of the evening became quieter, closer and more personal. There were hugs, kisses, playful fights, teasing and those private romantic moments that belong only to us.

Later, you dropped me downstairs, arranged an auto for me and we kept talking on the phone even after I reached home.

You had also bought a new shirt and showed it to me. And, of course, there was more teasing and playful hitting around the “bump” joke.

Chapter Three

One last evening, for now

15 September 2025 Directly to the room

The next day was different. There was no long journey before meeting you. I went directly to your room.

We knew that our time together was getting shorter.

You were going to the airport, and I was going to go home. So even the ordinary moments started feeling important.

We spent the evening together, trying to enjoy whatever time was left.

And somehow, the simplest moments became the ones I remember most.

One tiny moment

Chai, time running out, and you watching me

☕

We were making chai in the room that evening, knowing that our time together was almost over.

I was about to go home and you were about to leave for the airport.

I went into the bathroom to fix my hair, and you were outside watching me.

It was such a small moment. Nothing dramatic. But I liked that moment.

Maybe because when you really care about someone, even the smallest things become memories worth keeping.

Until the next time

Delhi to Pune, again

Then came the part neither of us likes — saying goodbye.

You had to leave for the airport, and I had to go home.

We knew it wasn't really an ending. It was just another Delhi–Pune goodbye.

Still, every goodbye carries a little heaviness with it when you know you are leaving someone you love.
