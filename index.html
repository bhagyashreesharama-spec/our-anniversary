OUR LITTLE DIARY

Kunnu & Bhagya

2024 — 2026

♡

A collection of the little moments that quietly became our everything.

INTRODUCTION

Before we knew
what this would become.

Every relationship has a beginning. Ours wasn't some perfectly planned movie scene.

It was simply two people meeting, talking, laughing, getting comfortable, and slowly becoming a little more important to each other.

And somehow, those ordinary moments became the memories we now want to keep forever.

♡

CHAPTER 01

The first date.

DATE 30 September 2024
DAY Monday
TIME 12:00 PM
PLACE Lodhi Garden
our beginning

30 September 2024.

The date that quietly became the beginning of us.

We met at Lodhi Garden around 12 in the afternoon.

And somewhere in between all the nervousness, awkwardness, conversations and little moments, something started changing.

You wanted to hug me. I refused.

Yes. I actually refused.

You left with that disappointed little face and went towards the airport.

But the story didn't end there.

We kept talking that night. And then we kept talking some more.

Somehow, without forcing anything, our conversations became fun, comfortable and full of little jokes.

It felt like we were developing this invisible but very jolly little bond.

We didn't know where it would go.

We just knew that we wanted to keep talking.

CHAPTER 02

The day we met again.

DATE 14 September 2025
DAY Sunday
TIME 3:00 PM
PLACE Green Park Metro Station
finally together again

I was busy with my planshow. I was already tired from everything, and your teasing finally made me come to meet you.

I was exhausted, but you were there… so I knew you'd take care of me.

We met at Green Park Metro Station around 3 PM.

I was nervous. You somehow made it easier.

Then came the metro ride.

I punched you, and of course people looked at us.

We went towards CP.

Before we even reached, you held my hand.

We went to a café, tried the food, and I basically decided that I did not like it.

"Let's leave."

You picked up my bag, booked an auto, and sat with me.

Then you started teasing me.

You kissed my cheek and I immediately became shy.

You put your arm around my waist and I told you not to.

Typical us.

♡

THAT EVENING

Just us.

When we reached the room, you showed me your Ganesh Chaturthi performance video.

We watched it, talked, teased each other, fought over little things, and laughed again.

I told you to stop using your phone.

Then came the hugs, kisses, talking, playful fights, and those quiet moments that belonged only to us.

new shirt teasing little fights too much talking

You had even bought a new shirt and showed it to me.

Somewhere between all the teasing and laughing, the day became another memory I didn't want to end.

CHAPTER 03

The next day.

DATE 15 September 2025
DAY Monday
PLACE His room
one more day

The next day, we met again.

We made Maggi and chai together.

We watched videos, talked about random things, and just spent time together.

Then you told me about how you got your job.

And somehow, that conversation became emotional.

You cried.

I hugged you.

And for a moment, everything else disappeared.

“

Not every important memory is a happy one.

We had a misunderstanding. I felt like you didn't love me.

I said it. I cried. You became emotional too.

And then there were hugs, tears, trying to understand each other, and trying to make things okay again.

Because that's us too.

We fight. We feel. We talk. We come back.

♡　♡　♡

Even our difficult moments became part of our story.

GOODBYE

And then you had to leave.

After all those moments, it was time to go home.

You had to leave for Pune.

I remember your face.

It looked like you didn't want to leave either.

And I cried.

Because after spending those days together, going back to being apart suddenly felt much harder.

Delhi and Pune were suddenly not just two cities.

They were the distance between two people who wanted to stay together.

FOR KUNNU ♡

A letter
for you.

Kunnu,

If I had to choose one word for these two years, I don't think I could.

There have been too many versions of us.

The silly us. The annoying us. The emotional us. The stubborn us. The laughing us. The missing-each-other us.

And somehow, I love that all of them are part of our story.

Thank you for being there through the good moments and the difficult ones.

Thank you for all the little things that probably seemed normal to you but became memories for me.

From that first date at Lodhi Garden to all the conversations, hugs, fights, chai, Maggi, laughter, tears, and goodbyes...

somehow, we reached two years.

And I still want more.

More memories. More silly conversations. More annoying each other. More making up. More growing together.

Happy two years, Kunnu.

Here's to whatever comes next.

— Bhagya
